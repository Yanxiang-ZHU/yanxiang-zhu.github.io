About

Bridging AI algorithms and silicon

I am a first-year M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University, working with Prof. Zishen Wan in the Wan Lab on hardware-software co-design for world action models (world models and vision-language-action models). My work sits where digital IC design meets AI systems: as an undergraduate at Fudan University I built stochastic neural network accelerators, RISC-V processors and EDA tools, and during my internship at Mammoth Supercomputing Semiconductor I designed the top-level architecture and RTL compute data path of a CNN super-resolution inference ASIC.

I enjoy problems that cross the hardware-software boundary — profiling models, designing the memory hierarchy they run on, and closing the loop with FPGA-based pre-silicon validation. Outside the lab you will find me on the track: I run the 400 meters and won a gold and a silver medal with the Fudan 4×400m relay team.
